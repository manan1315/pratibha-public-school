# Admin Panel — User Guide
### Pratibha Public School Basna — Website Management

---

## 1. How to open the Admin Panel

Open any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) on any device — computer, laptop, tablet or mobile — and go to:

```
https://YOUR-WEBSITE-ADDRESS/admin
```

**Example:** if your website is `www.ppsbasna.com`, then the admin panel is
`www.ppsbasna.com/admin`

> Tip: Save this link as a bookmark so you don't have to type it every time.

---

## 2. Login

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Email | `admin@ppsbasna.com` |
| Password | *(the password given to you)* |

Type both and click **Sign In**.

You stay logged in for 24 hours. After that, simply log in again.

---

## 3. What you can manage

After logging in you will see a menu on the left side:

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Overview — how many enquiries, news items, photos, visitors |
| **Sliders** | The big rotating photos on the homepage |
| **About** | Text of Vision, Mission, Ethos, Welcome message |
| **Faculty** | Teacher list with photos and qualifications |
| **News** | News articles and announcements |
| **Gallery** | Photo albums and pictures |
| **Enquiries** | Admission enquiries submitted by parents |
| **Testimonials** | Parent and student reviews |
| **Achievements** | Student awards, results, prizes |
| **Facilities** | Labs, library, transport, etc. |
| **FAQs** | Questions and answers on the Admissions page |
| **Announcements** | The scrolling yellow strip at the top of the site |
| **Subscribers** | Emails collected from the newsletter box |
| **Bus Routes** | Route numbers, stops and timings |
| **Downloads** | PDF forms parents can download |
| **Popups** | The pop-up message that appears on the homepage |
| **Settings** | School name, phone numbers, address, social media links |
| **Users** | Change your admin password |

---

## 4. Adding something new (example: a news article)

1. Click **News** in the left menu
2. Click the blue **Add New** button (top right)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — headline of the news
   - **Content** — the full text
   - **Featured Image** — click **Upload**, choose a photo from your device
   - **Date** — pick the date
   - **Category** — choose from the list
   - **Show on homepage** — tick this to display it on the homepage
4. Click **Create**
5. Open the website and refresh — your news is live

**Editing** — click the blue pencil icon next to any item.
**Deleting** — click the red bin icon. It will ask for confirmation first.

---

## 5. Adding photos to the Gallery

1. Click **Gallery**
2. To make a new album: click **New Album**, give it a name, click **Create**
3. Click **Manage Photos** on the album
4. Click **Upload Photos** — you can select **many photos at once**
5. Type a caption under any photo (it saves automatically when you click away)

---

## 6. Viewing admission enquiries

1. Click **Enquiries**
2. New enquiries appear highlighted with a red **NEW** label
3. Click the envelope icon to see full details and the parent's message
4. Add a private note (e.g. "Called on 12 Dec, visiting Saturday") and click **Save & mark read**
5. Click **Export CSV** to download all enquiries as an Excel-compatible file

---

## 7. Changing school contact details

1. Click **Settings**
2. Update the phone numbers, address, email, social media links
3. Click **Save All Settings**

These changes appear everywhere on the website — header, footer, contact page.

---

## 8. Changing your password

1. Click **Users** in the left menu
2. Enter your current password, then the new password twice
3. Click **Update Password**

Use a strong password and do not share it.

---

## 9. Important notes

- **Images:** keep photos under 5 MB. JPG, PNG and WebP work best.
- **Documents:** PDF is recommended for forms and notices.
- **Hide instead of delete:** most sections have a "Show on website" tick box. Un-tick it to hide something without losing it.
- **Nothing breaks:** if you delete something by mistake, just add it again — the website never goes down because of an edit.

---
